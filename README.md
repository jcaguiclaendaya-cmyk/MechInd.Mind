# ⚙️ MechInd Mind

**MechInd Mind** is a **free, community-driven academic platform** designed specifically for **Mechanical Engineering (ME)** and **Industrial Engineering (IE)** students. It serves as a centralized hub where students can **search, access, and contribute problem sets with expanded, human-written solutions**—all without spending a single peso.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

ME and IE students often struggle to find reliable practice problems with clear, detailed solutions. Paid platforms like Chegg are expensive, and free resources are scattered and unreliable. **MechInd Mind** solves this by providing:

- A **searchable database** of ME and IE problem sets
- **Expanded, step-by-step solutions** with explanations, diagrams, and tips
- A **community-driven** platform where students help students
- **100% FREE** access—no subscriptions, no hidden fees

---

## 🔥 Key Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 📚 **Search Problems** | Search by subject, topic, difficulty, year level, or school |
| 📖 **Expanded Solutions** | Step-by-step solutions with diagrams, common mistakes, and tips |
| 👥 **Community-Driven** | Students and professors contribute original problems and solutions |
| 📊 **Progress Tracking** | Monitor solved problems, streaks, and weak areas |
| 🏆 **Gamification** | Earn badges, points, and certificates for achievements |
| 💬 **Collaboration** | Discuss problems and join study groups |
| 📱 **Accessible** | Mobile-friendly with dark mode and downloadable PDFs |
