📖 About MechInd Mind

Empowering ME & IE students, one problem at a time.

🎯

What We Do

MechInd Mind is a free, community-driven academic platform designed specifically for Mechanical Engineering (ME) and Industrial Engineering (IE) students. We provide access to quality problem sets with expanded, human-written solutions – completely free.

🚀

Our Mission

To empower ME and IE students by providing free access to quality practice problems, detailed solutions, and a collaborative learning community – because every student deserves the resources to succeed.

🌟

Our Vision

To become the go-to academic hub for ME and IE students across the country – a place where students help students master engineering, one problem at a time.
