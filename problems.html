Thermodynamics – Ideal Gas Law

Difficulty: Medium | Year: 2nd Year | 45 solves

A rigid tank contains 5 kg of an ideal gas at 300 kPa and 400 K. The gas is heated until the temperature reaches 600 K. Determine the final pressure.

IE

Operations Research – Linear Programming

Difficulty: Hard | Year: 3rd Year | 32 solves

A company produces two products. Product A requires 2 hours of labor and 1 hour of machine time. Product B requires 1 hour of labor and 3 hours of machine time. The company has 100 labor hours and 80 machine hours available daily. Profit per unit of A is $40 and B is $30. How many units of each should be produced to maximize profit?

ME

Fluid Mechanics – Bernoulli's Equation

Difficulty: Easy | Year: 1st Year | 67 solves

Water flows through a pipe with a velocity of 3 m/s. The pressure at point A is 150 kPa. At point B, the pipe narrows to half the diameter. Determine the velocity and pressure at point B. Assume no losses.

IE

Industrial Statistics – Control Charts

Difficulty: Medium | Year: 2nd Year | 28 solves

A manufacturing process produces shafts with a target diameter of 50 mm. Sample data shows a mean of 50.2 mm and a standard deviation of 0.5 mm. Construct an X-bar control chart with 3-sigma limits. Determine if the process is in control.

ME

Strength of Materials – Simple Stress

Difficulty: Easy | Year: 1st Year | 52 solves

A steel rod with a diameter of 20 mm and a length of 2 meters is subjected to an axial tensile load of 50 kN. Determine the stress in the rod.

IE

Engineering Economy – Break-Even Analysis

Difficulty: Medium | Year: 3rd Year | 21 solves

A company is considering a new project. Fixed costs are $50,000, variable cost per unit is $10, and selling price per unit is $25. How many units must be sold to break even?
